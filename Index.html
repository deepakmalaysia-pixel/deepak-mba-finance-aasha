A Skills Roadmap for MBA Finance Graduates, India

An MBA gets you
the interview.
These get you the offer.

Most MBA Finance graduates leave campus knowing the theory of markets, accounting, and valuation — but not the tools recruiters actually test for: a live Excel model, a Tally-ready GST return, a Power BI dashboard, a NISM certificate, a case cracked in fifteen minutes.

This roadmap is organised the way the finance job market actually is: by role, not by subject. Pick the ticker that matches where you want to sit — trading floor, wealth desk, analytics team, audit firm, consulting bay, or corporate finance office — and follow it one quarter at a time.

Q1 · Foundation

Read the numbers before you trade them

This roadmap organises finance careers the way hiring managers actually think about them — by role first, tools second. Each track is a self-contained, four-quarter path built entirely on free or low-cost, widely available resources: regulator and industry-body material (NISM, AMFI, ICAI, GST portal, Income Tax Department), free courseware from Tally and Microsoft, and well-known public YouTube channels.
